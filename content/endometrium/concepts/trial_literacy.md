## Fact

Endometrial cancer recommendations are shaped by trials that may use different populations, endpoints, risk definitions and treatment sequences.

## Why It Matters

To understand a recommendation, you must know what question the trial asked. A positive PFS result in advanced disease does not automatically answer an adjuvant RT question.

## Clinical Consequence

Trial literacy prevents overgeneralization. It helps separate localized adjuvant treatment logic from recurrent/metastatic systemic therapy logic.

## RCP Consequence

When citing a trial, ask:

- population;
- stage/setting;
- biomarker selection;
- treatment arms;
- endpoint;
- magnitude of benefit;
- toxicity;
- relevance to this patient.

## Common Trap

Memorizing trial names without knowing their clinical question.

## Micro-Question

Why is a trial in recurrent/metastatic disease not automatically applicable to postoperative adjuvant treatment?

Expected reasoning: disease burden, intent, endpoints, competing risks and treatment goals differ.

## Prerequisites

- basic statistics
- PFS/OS/HR interpretation
- treatment setting definitions
