# p53 anormal

Status: needs_review

## Fact

p53-abnormal endometrial carcinoma is a molecular category associated with aggressive biology and often overlaps with serous-like behavior.

## Why It Matters

p53 status can help identify high-risk tumors that may need intensified adjuvant reasoning, including systemic therapy and radiotherapy combinations depending on stage and other factors.

## Clinical Consequence

p53-abnormal status can shift the interpretation of a tumor from anatomically limited to biologically high risk.

## RCP Consequence

When p53 is abnormal, check:

- histology and grade;
- stage and myometrial/cervical involvement;
- LVSI;
- nodal staging;
- HER2 status when serous carcinoma is present or suspected, depending on local protocol;
- role of chemotherapy and RT.

## Common Trap

Thinking early anatomical stage always means low biological risk. p53-abnormal disease can break that intuition.

## Micro-Question

Why can molecular class change the adjuvant discussion even when the tumor appears uterus-confined?

Expected reasoning: anatomical extent and biological aggressiveness are different risk dimensions; both inform relapse probability and treatment intensity.

## Prerequisites

- molecular classification
- histologic subtypes
- adjuvant risk stratification
