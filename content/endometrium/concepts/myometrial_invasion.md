# Invasion myométriale

Status: needs_review

## Fact

Myometrial invasion describes how deeply the tumor infiltrates the uterine muscle wall.

## Why It Matters

Depth of invasion is a proxy for local aggressiveness and access to lymphovascular/nodal dissemination pathways. A tumor limited to the endometrium does not have the same risk profile as a tumor invading deeply into the myometrium.

## Clinical Consequence

Myometrial invasion contributes to staging and postoperative risk stratification. It also interacts with grade, LVSI, histology and molecular class.

## RCP Consequence

The RCP should verify:

- depth of myometrial invasion;
- method of assessment: pathology after surgery or MRI preoperatively;
- associated LVSI;
- cervical stromal involvement;
- nodal staging status.

## Common Trap

Remembering only "<50% vs ≥50%" without understanding why: deeper invasion often means higher probability of lymphovascular access and extrauterine relapse risk.

## Micro-Question

Why does deep myometrial invasion influence nodal-risk reasoning?

Expected reasoning: deeper invasion increases the chance that tumor has reached vascular and lymphatic structures that connect to pelvic and para-aortic pathways.

## Prerequisites

- uterine anatomy
- uterine histology
- lymphatic drainage
