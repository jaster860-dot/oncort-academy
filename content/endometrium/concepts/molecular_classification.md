# Classification moléculaire dans le cancer de l'endomètre

Status: needs_review

## Fact

Modern endometrial cancer classification integrates molecular groups such as POLE-mutated, mismatch repair deficient, p53-abnormal and no specific molecular profile.

## Why It Matters

Molecular class can modify prognosis and treatment intensity. It helps avoid treating every tumor only by old morphology-based categories.

## Clinical Consequence

The same apparent stage and grade can carry different relapse risks depending on molecular class. Molecular classification also connects localized management with recurrent/metastatic systemic options, especially for dMMR/MSI disease.

## RCP Consequence

Before finalizing adjuvant strategy, the RCP should ask:

- Is MMR status known?
- Is p53 status known?
- Is POLE status needed or available?
- Does the molecular result reclassify risk?
- Does it affect systemic treatment options now or at relapse?

## Common Trap

Using molecular markers as trivia. They are not just labels; they can alter risk group, staging interpretation and systemic treatment logic.

## Micro-Question

Why is molecular classification especially important in high-grade endometrial cancer?

Expected reasoning: high-grade morphology alone does not fully define prognosis; molecular subtype can identify biologically distinct groups with different risk and treatment implications.

## Prerequisites

- tumor biology basics
- histologic subtype
- grade
- FIGO 2023 staging
